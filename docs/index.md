# Maxime Mirka

Data Scientist / AI expert, PhD. I work at the intersection of data science and artificial intelligence, with experience spanning academic research and industrial applications.

This site brings together projects from machine learning and AI systems to data engineering and applied data science. It includes my doctoral research, thesis and supporting material, as well as my resume and teaching domains.

## About

I am a Data Scientist with a background in robotics engineering and academic research. I completed my PhD at the Université de Montpellier (LIRMM), where I worked on machine learning for adaptive control and the design of computing systems (thesis/index.md).

Since 2022, I have been working full-time in industry, applying data science and AI to real-world problems while continuing to explore new methods and technologies.

This portfolio brings together both sides of that experience: research-driven experimentation and practical data science, with an emphasis on understanding problems, building reliable solutions, and making complex systems useful.

## The PhD, in three projects

### [Online RL control of OpenMP energy efficiency](projects/omp-energy-rl.md)

Reinforcement learning picking core count and clock frequency for a parallel
program *while it runs*, from a progress metric read inside the OpenMP runtime.

*[Thesis chapter 4](thesis/04-openmp-energy-efficiency.md); [ReCoSoC 2019 and MOCAST 2020](publications/index.md).*
</div>

<div markdown>
![A grid of generated 9-router network topologies drawn as graphs](assets/figures/gannoc/topology_grid_graph.png)

### [GANNoC](projects/gannoc.md)

Network-on-chip topology design cast as image generation: a 9×9 binary
adjacency matrix learned by a WGAN-GP and steered by a frozen reward network toward specific properties.

*[Thesis chapter 5](thesis/05-gannoc.md); [RAPIDO 2021](publications/index.md).*
</div>

<div markdown>
![A grid of 8×8 heat maps showing router-class assignments across a parameter sweep](assets/figures/m-rwgan/heatmap_panel_grid.png)

### [RWGAN](projects/m-rwgan.md)

The same mechanism generalized to several rewards 
(illustrated with three, for throughput, power and area), 
assigning a buffer-size class 
to each of 64 routers in a fixed 8x8 mesh topology.

*[Thesis chapter 6](thesis/06-m-rwgan.md); [DATE 2022](publications/index.md).*
</div>
